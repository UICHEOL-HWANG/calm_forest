CALM FOREST · BEGINNER'S GUIDE

Welcome to calm forest, a guide for first-timers

You arrive in a forest village that has lost its spark. Chop trees, tend fields, build a home, and bring the withered World Tree back to bloom. This guide walks you through your first 30 minutes with real in-game screens.

Title screen · PC browsers, phones, and the iPhone and Android apps · as of October 2026 (screens shown in Korean)

QUICK GUIDE 1 · FIRST 30 MINUTES

Do these six things and you know the basics

The village is almost empty at first. Follow these steps in order and the merchant, the carpenter, and the grandpa come back one by one, and the way to save the World Tree opens. The later chapters expand on these six steps.

1 🪓 Chop three trees

Stand next to a tree and you pick up the axe on your own. Press Space (the big button on phones) 3 times to fell it for +3 wood.

2 🧙 Call the merchant for a first trade

Hold 5 wood, 1 fish, or 4 crops and the merchant walks over to buy it at a high price. The shop and price board open after that.

3 🌰 One plot, seed to harvest

Till with the hoe → plant a seed → water 2 times → harvest with the sickle.

4 🔨 Build a house

Hammer at the house site. Lay the deck and the Carpenter moves in. 60 wood · 25 stone · 80🪙 gets you to the roof.

5 🌉 Fix the bridge and meet the grandpa

Once your house is done you can fix the broken bridge for 10 wood · 5 stone. Cross the creek to the museum island and meet The Curator.

6 🌳 Revive the World Tree's first branch

Open the World Tree board at the base of the tree in the middle of the village and decorate the clearing of the 💧 Water Branch. The Old Fisherman and the river come back.

There's no rush. You start with 0 coins, so chopping first is the quickest start. Everything else can be done in whatever order you like. The game saves itself every 30 seconds.
QUICK GUIDE 2 · CONTROLS

Two things to remember: move and act

Using tools, talking, opening doors, and picking things up are all the same button: Space on PC, the big round button at the bottom right on phones.

🖥️ PC · keyboard and mouse

W A S D to move (arrow keys work too)
Space to act · close prompts
1 to switch tool sets
2~6 to pick a tool in the current set
C to sit · R to rotate furniture
Mouse wheel to zoom · Shift+wheel to tilt the camera

📱 Phone · touch

Drag the joystick at the bottom left to move
The big button at the bottom right is your action
Tap the tool button next to it: your tools fan out. Tap the one you want
Near a villager or a door, a 💬 bubble pops up beside your character. Tap it to talk or go in
Pinch with two fingers to zoom, slide them up or down together to tilt

Tools often switch by themselves. Next to a tree you hold the 🪓 axe, at the lake the 🎣 rod, at the house site the 🔨 hammer. Where no tool is needed, like the cafe, indoors, or the woods, you carry them on your back.
There's no run key. Eat a 🥘 vegetable porridge or 🍄 mushroom soup instead and you move 40% faster for a while.

Open the site and pick an animal friend

On PC we recommend Chrome. On phones you can play in the browser or get the App Store or Google Play app.

A guest village doesn't carry over to another device. If you plan to keep playing, sign in with Google or email. You can change your character later from the ☰ menu; your house, fields, and collection stay as they are.

05 · FIRST 10 MINUTES

Three trees bring your first visitor

You start with 0 coins, so the very first thing to do is chop trees.

  1. 1Stand next to a tree and you pick up the 🪓 axe on your own (on PC you can also open the 🏕️ outdoor set with 1 and take it with 2).
  2. 2Right next to the tree, press Space 3 times. You get +3 wood only when it falls. It grows back after 30 seconds.
  3. 3Once you have 5 wood, the 🧙 Wandering Merchant walks over. "Sell 5 wood +30🪙": three times the usual price. Holding 1 fish (+25🪙) or 4 crops (+25🪙) first works too. It's a one-time welcome trade.
  4. 4After that first trade, the 🛒 stall to the east and the 📊 price board open. Buy 🌰 seeds or 🌱 fertilizer, or just save up.
Weather and food help. On ❄️ snowy days branches snap easily and you get more wood, and eating a 🍱 lunchbox gives +1 wood every time you fell a tree.

06 · FARMING

Till → plant → water → cut

You start with 8 seeds. Every harvest returns +1 crop and +2 seeds, so you'll almost never run short.

  1. 1⛏️ Hoe: Space on empty ground. You can't make plots near trees, the lake, or the house site. Move a little.
  2. 2🌰 Seeds: Space on a tilled plot. One of carrot, tomato, blueberry, or pumpkin grows at random.
  3. 3💧 Watering can: wet soil won't take water for 9 seconds. When the soil dries, water once more, 2 times in all.
  4. 4🌾 Sickle: when the "Harvest!" marker shows, press Space.
Leave the soil dry for 1 minute and the crop wilts (🥀). Re-till a wilted plot with the hoe. On 🌧️ rainy days crops grow without watering, and 🌱 fertilizer (20🪙) makes them harvestable right away.
🪏 Removing a plot: if you tilled the wrong spot or the village is covered in plots, take the shovel (6) from the 🌾 farming set and press Space twice in front of an empty plot. The first dig roughs up the furrows and shows "Dig once more to remove the plot"; you have 6 seconds to dig again (after that it goes back to normal). Plots with crops must be harvested or cleared with the hoe first. Filled-in spots sometimes drop 📖 collection items from "Underground".

08 · BUILDING YOUR HOUSE

From deck to roof with a hammer

It's the "🏠 Build Your House Here" spot with the green circle, northwest of the village. Stand on the house site and you pick up the 🔨 hammer on your own.

  1. 1Press Space inside the circle. Deck (15 wood) → log walls (20 wood · 10 stone) → roof (25 wood · 15 stone · 80🪙), three stages. Lay the deck and the 👷 Carpenter moves into the village. Stone comes from the ⛏️ cave to the west; coins from selling wood and crops.
  2. 2Roof on means done! You earn the 🏠 "Home Sweet Home" badge (+20🪙) and you can fix the broken bridge over the creek (chapter 11).
  3. 3Space at the door → inside. Use 🎨 Decorate to place furniture. Furniture costs 🥕 crops, not coins (only the fish tanks cost 🐟 fish). R rotates.
  4. 4Outside the house you can change roof, wall, and door colors and expand. Stage 4 🧱 Brick Loft (45 wood · 15 stone · 120🪙) → stage 5 🏢 Penthouse (75 wood · 30 stone · 10 coal · 350🪙) → stage 6 🏝️ Rooftop Villa (120 wood · 50 stone · 3 gems · 800🪙) → stage 7 🏡 Garden Estate (180 wood · 80 stone · 5 gems · 1500🪙). For stage 7 you pick 🌸 Terrace Court or 🏮 Hanok Courtyard, and that choice stays. As it grows you get an attic, a second floor, and a rooftop, and big furniture bought with coins opens up.
Colors unlock as you play. Finishing or expanding the house, and now and then fishing, harvesting, or requests, unlock new roof, wall, and door colors. Exterior decor and 🧩 add-ons are in chapter 23.

09 · LAKE FISHING

Cast, wait, and press once more on the bite

At the lake southeast of the village, from the wooden pier. Stand at the lakeside and you pick up the 🎣 rod on your own.

  1. 1Press Space at the water's edge → the bobber flies out.
  2. 2After a few seconds, "❗ A bite! Reel it in now!" appears. On rainy days they bite sooner.
  3. 3Press Space again right away. Even a little late and it gets away 🐟💨. The time you get to reel in varies a little with every cast.
  4. 4Pressing again while waiting reels the line back in. Switching tools or walking away also cancels.

11 · WORLD TREE · BRIDGE · REVIVING THE VILLAGE

Bring the withered World Tree back to bloom

The big tree in the middle of the village is the World Tree. As its strength returns, villagers and places come back one by one. This is the big goal of the game.

  1. 1🌉 Fix the bridge: once your house is done, "🌉 Fix the bridge (🪵10 🪨5)" appears at the broken bridge over the creek. Fix it, cross to the museum island, and meet 🧑‍🦳 The Curator. That finishes story chapter 1 (+40🪙).
  2. 2🌳 Open the World Tree board: press Space (the big button on phones) at the base of the World Tree. Before you meet the grandpa, it only says "🌳 Withered World Tree · go find the grandpa".
  3. 3🏡 Decorate the clearing: each branch has a clearing with 6 slots. Tap a decoration card below (a little pond for 3 stone, a big tree for 2 wood, and so on) to pay for it and place it in the next empty slot; tap a placed tile to remove it and get the materials back. Fill the goals on the bar above (for example 💧 Moisture 6 · 🌑 Shade 2) and 🍃 leaves sprout and that branch's villager comes back.
  4. 4🎁 Offer bundles: once a clearing is done, its branch opens bundles. For example the 💧 River Bundle takes 5 fish · 5 stone (→ 30🪙) and the dock bundle takes 20 wood · 10 stone (→ 40🪙).

12 · MINE · WORKBENCH · FREE KITCHEN · CARVING

Dig materials, craft, and cook

⛏️ Mining cave black rock entrance, west of the village · from the start

Hit a glittering vein 3 times with the hoe. Normally you get stone and coal; 💎 gems only turn up on 🌫️ foggy days. Veins respawn after 14 seconds. Stone and coal go into house expansions and World Tree decorations; gems sell for 40🪙. Leave with Space at the "Exit" sign.

🔧 Workbench 4 tabs · from the start

Permanent tool upgrades · outdoor decorations (scarecrow, fence, flower bed) · gifts for villagers · 🗿 carving. Carving orders change daily, 3 a day. Tap and drag to chip away the bright pieces and leave only the dark outline; the cleaner the cut, the bigger the reward (22 to 30🪙 × grade).

🍳 Free Kitchen east of the workbench · Kitchen Branch

11 recipes. Hand over ingredients and play a minigame (boiling · chopping · grilling · seasoning). Dishes with more ★ chain several rounds, and the better you do, the longer the buff lasts.
Start with vegetable porridge (3 crops) = +40% speed for 60s. The full table and the 🧺 pantry are in chapter 20.

13 · FOUR PLACES TO THE SOUTH

Café · coop · Foraging Woods · Firefly Valley

South of the village are four spots where coins and materials pile up. They open one by one as you revive the World Tree's branches (chapter 11).

☕ Café Kitchen Branch · red-roofed building to the south

Four guests order through bubbles over their heads. Press Space beside them: a dish from your pantry goes out right away, and if you have the ingredients it's cooked on the spot. 28 to 78🪙 a dish (tastier pays more), and a 60🪙 bonus for serving all four.

🐔 Chicken Coop Field Branch · southwest of the farm entrance

Unlocks after visiting 2 days in a row (25 wood · 10 stone · 60🪙). Feed 2 seeds and get 2 🥚 eggs the next day. Sell for 6🪙 each or use them in an omelette.

🍄 Foraging Woods Forest Branch · signpost to the southwest

No tools needed. Press Space next to mushrooms, wild berries, acorns, or herbs to pick them. They regrow in 1 to 2 minutes. Three foraged items make 🍄 mushroom soup (90s of fast feet).

🌟 Firefly Valley Forest Branch · south of the café · night only

Swing the 🦋 bug net at the moment a firefly flashes brightest for a 90% catch, otherwise 35%. Collect all 4 kinds for the 🌟 "Night Collector" badge (+40🪙).

14 · FAR-OFF ADVENTURES

Four places beyond the village, once you've settled in

Press Space at the signposts or entrances on the village edge to go in. Each one opens at a different time.

🛶 Boat Dock Water Branch · due north, 12 o'clock

Ride the rowboat down the river for about 60 seconds. The boat moves on its own; you steer left and right, Space is a sprint. Dodge rocks and logs, collect ⭐ star shards. 💡 3 lamps are your lives. Three runs a day, and the course is the same for everyone that day. Spend your star shards at the 🧰 Boatman's Shed on the dock to upgrade the oars, hull, and lantern. A 🧰 treasure chest also floats down once a day (chapter 27).

🌊 Seaside Pier Water Branch bundles · lighthouse to the northeast

A tug of war with big fish. On 🔴 Hold on…! press nothing; on 🟢 Reel it in!! mash. If you're dragged to the red plank at the end of the pier, it escapes. Mackerel (day) · yellowtail · sunfish (night) · ⚔️ tuna (one a day). After weighing it you let it go and get 1 to 3 fish and 4 to 24🪙.

🌫️ Misty Forest Forest Branch · old-tree arch to the northwest

Start at the 🌳 Guardian Tree (a practice round first if it's your first time). When shadow spirits approach, slow them with the 🏮 lantern and tap when the ♪ mark is at its smallest to soothe them. It's music, not a fight, so failing costs nothing.

🏔️ Snow Mountain from your second day · northwest of the village

"🏔️ Climb the mountain" in front of it. Walk up the ridge picking up 🌰 acorns, then sled down from the top. A warm hut cafe waits at the summit. Details are in chapter 24.

Frequently asked questions

My tools disappearedIn the woods, indoors, and at the cafe your tools go on your back automatically. Step outside and they come back, or press 1 (tap the tool button on phones).
I can't make a plotNot near trees, the lake, the house site, or the workbench. Move a step or two, or go to My Farm.
I want to remove a plotTake the 🪏 shovel (6) from the 🌾 farming set and dig an empty plot twice within 6 seconds. If it has a crop, harvest it or clear it with the hoe first.
