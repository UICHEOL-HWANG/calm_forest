CALM FOREST · BEGINNER'S GUIDE

Welcome to calm forest, a guide for first-timers

A cozy forest village where you chop trees, tend fields, fish, and build your own house. This guide walks you through your first 30 minutes with real in-game screens.

Title screen · works on PC browsers and phones · as of September 2026 (screens shown in Korean)

QUICK GUIDE 1 · FIRST 30 MINUTES

Do these six things and you know the basics

Follow them in order and coins, fields, a house, and fishing all open up at once. The later chapters expand on these six steps.

1 🪓 Chop three trees

Pick the axe from the outdoor tool set and press Space 3 times. Felling a tree gives +3 wood.

2 🧙 Sell to the visiting merchant

Once you hold 5 wood, the merchant walks over to you. +30🪙, three times the usual price, one time only.

3 🌰 One plot, seed to harvest

Till with the hoe → plant a seed → water 2 times → harvest with the sickle.

4 🎣 One fish from the lake

Cast from the pier. When "A bite!" appears, press Space again right away.

5 🔨 Build a house and step inside

Hammer inside the circle three times. 60 wood · 25 stone · 80🪙 gets you to the roof.

6 🎨 Place one piece of furniture

Furniture costs crops, not coins. A rug is 2 crops.

There's no rush. You start with 0 coins, so chopping first is the quickest way to get going. Everything else can be done in whatever order you like. The game saves itself every 30 seconds.
QUICK GUIDE 2 · CONTROLS

Only two things to remember, move and act

Using a tool, talking, opening doors, and picking things up are all the same key. On PC it's Space; on a phone it's the one round button at the bottom right.

05 · FIRST 10 MINUTES

Three trees earn your first coins

You start with 0 coins, so the very first job is chopping.

  1. 1Press 1 to open the 🏕️ outdoor set and 2 to take the axe.
  2. 2Stand right next to a tree and press Space 3 times. Wood drops only when it falls: +3 wood. It regrows after 30 seconds.
  3. 3Once you hold 5 wood, the 🧙 Wandering Merchant walks over. "Sell 5 wood for +30🪙", three times the usual price.
  4. 4Now you have 30 coins. Buy 🌰 seeds or 🌱 fertilizer at the east stall, or just save up.
Weather and food make it pay better. On ❄️ snowy days branches snap easily and drop more wood, and a 🍱 Picnic Lunchbox gives +1 wood per felled tree.

06 · FARMING

Till → plant → water → cut

You start with 8 seeds. Every harvest returns +1 crop and +2 seeds, so you'll almost never run short.

  1. 1⛏️ Hoe: Space on empty ground. You can't make plots near trees, the lake, or the house site. Move a little.
  2. 2🌰 Seeds: Space on a tilled plot. One of carrot, tomato, blueberry, or pumpkin grows at random.
  3. 3💧 Watering can: wet soil won't take water for 9 seconds. When the soil dries, water once more, 2 times in all.
  4. 4🌾 Sickle: when the "Harvest!" marker shows, press Space.
Leave the soil dry for 1 minute and the crop wilts (🥀). Re-till a wilted plot with the hoe. On 🌧️ rainy days crops grow without watering, and 🌱 fertilizer (20🪙) makes them harvestable right away.
🪏 Removing a plot: if you tilled the wrong spot or the village is covered in plots, take the shovel (6) from the 🌾 farming set and press Space twice in front of an empty plot. The first dig roughs up the furrows and shows "Dig once more to remove the plot"; you have 6 seconds to dig again (after that it goes back to normal). Plots with crops must be harvested or cleared with the hoe first. Filled-in spots sometimes drop 📖 collection items from "Underground".

As it grows you get an attic, a second floor, and a rooftop, and big furniture bought with coins opens up.

09 · LAKE FISHING

Cast, wait, and press once more on the bite

At the lake southeast of the village, from the wooden pier. You can't walk into the water.

  1. 1Take the 🎣 rod (4) and press Space at the water's edge → the bobber flies out.
  2. 2After a few seconds, "❗ A bite! Reel it in now!" appears. On rainy days they bite sooner.
  3. 3Press Space again right away. Even a little late and it gets away 🐟💨. The time you get to reel in varies a little with every cast.
  4. 4Pressing again while waiting reels the line back in. Switching tools or walking away also cancels.

11 · MINE · WORKBENCH · FREE KITCHEN · CARVING

Dig materials, craft, and cook

⛏️ Mining cave black rock entrance, west of the village

Hit a glittering vein 3 times with the hoe. Normally you get stone and coal; 💎 gems only turn up on 🌫️ foggy days. Veins respawn after 14 seconds. Stone and coal go into house expansions and the big pot; gems sell for 40🪙. Leave with Space at the "Exit" sign.

🔧 Workbench 4 tabs

Permanent tool upgrades · outdoor decorations (scarecrow, fence, flower bed) · gifts for villagers · 🗿 carving. Carving orders change daily, 3 a day. Tap and drag to chip away the bright pieces and leave only the dark outline; the cleaner the cut, the bigger the reward (22 to 30🪙 × grade).

🍳 Free Kitchen east of the workbench

11 recipes. Hand over ingredients and play a minigame (boiling · chopping · grilling · seasoning). Dishes with more ★ chain several rounds, and the better you do, the longer the buff lasts.
Start with vegetable porridge (3 crops) = +40% speed for 60s. The full table and the 🧺 pantry are in chapter 19.

12 · FOUR PLACES TO THE SOUTH

Café · coop · Foraging Woods · Firefly Valley

South of the village are four spots where coins and materials pile up. Many need no tools at all.

☕ Café red-roofed building to the south

Four guests order through bubbles over their heads. Press Space beside them: a dish from your pantry goes out right away, and if you have the ingredients it's cooked on the spot. 28 to 78🪙 a dish (tastier pays more), and a 60🪙 bonus for serving all four.

🐔 Chicken Coop southwest of the farm entrance

Unlocks after visiting 2 days in a row (25 wood · 10 stone · 60🪙). Feed 2 seeds and get 2 🥚 eggs the next day. Sell for 6🪙 each or use them in an omelette.

🍄 Foraging Woods signpost to the southwest

No tools needed. Press Space next to mushrooms, wild berries, acorns, or herbs to pick them. They regrow in 1 to 2 minutes. Three foraged items make 🍄 mushroom soup (90s of fast feet).

🌟 Firefly Valley south of the café · night only

Swing the 🦋 bug net at the moment a firefly flashes brightest for a 90% catch, otherwise 35%. Collect all 4 kinds for the 🌟 "Night Collector" badge (+40🪙).

13 · FAR-OFF ADVENTURES

Three places beyond the village, once you've settled in

All three are open from the start. Press Space at the signposts on the village edge to enter.

🌊 Seaside Pier lighthouse to the northeast

A tug of war with big fish. On 🔴 Hold on…! press nothing; on 🟢 Reel it in!! mash. If you're dragged to the red plank at the end of the pier, it escapes. Mackerel (day) · yellowtail · sunfish (night) · ⚔️ tuna (one a day). You weigh them and let them go.

🛶 Boat Dock due north, 12 o'clock

Ride the rowboat down the river for about 60 seconds. The boat moves on its own; you steer left and right, Space is a sprint. Dodge rocks and logs, collect ⭐ star shards. 💡 3 lamps are your lives. Three runs a day, and the course is the same for everyone that day. Spend your star shards at the 🧰 Boatman's Shed on the dock to upgrade the oars, hull, and lantern.

🌫️ Misty Forest old-tree arch to the northwest

Start at the 🌳 Guardian Tree (a practice round first if it's your first time). When shadow spirits approach, slow them with the 🏮 lantern and tap when the ♪ mark is at its smallest to soothe them. It's music, not a fight, so failing costs nothing.

🍎 Orchard hill east of the village

After your first harvest, the 🧑‍🌾 Farmer hands you 🌾 wheat seeds. Harvest the wheat (a premium crop) and the orchard opens, and you get 2 🍎 Apple Saplings. Saplings are sold at the 🛒 Shop (Apple 🪙40 to Chestnut 🪙130). You can plant up to 10 trees, and the four spots by the stream never need watering. Talking to a villager opens their villager window first. Press 💬 Talk there for a chat that starts with a weather greeting and goes back and forth three times. You pick each reply from three choices. You can chat with each villager up to twice a day.

Chatting has no reward. It's just time to hear your neighbors' stories. The day's lines are the same for everyone, so you can ask a friend, "What did the Farmer say today?"
